## Step 1 to 3. Two-week theatre template
Template: standard. Each robot has 14 days x 3 sessions. Standard hours are weekday AM and PM (Friday PM maintenance); evening and weekend sessions count only when used.
Booked sessions per fortnight = 18; available = 18; utilisation = 18 / 18 = 100%.
Annual cases = cases per fortnight x 22.5 fortnights x 100% going ahead; then limited to demand (check on) and to 700 per robot.

## Guardrail warnings
- Nephrectomy and partial nephrectomy: the template schedules 68 cases a year but demand is 50, so its lists would be only 74% full.
- Endometriosis excision: the template schedules 135 cases a year but demand is 40, so its lists would be only 30% full.
- Adnexal surgery: the template schedules 45 cases a year but demand is 30, so its lists would be only 67% full.
- Abdominal wall reconstruction: 35 cases a year are not scheduled (45 of 80 scheduled); the template does not schedule enough cases.
- Upper GI (anti-reflux and hiatal hernia): 20 cases a year are not scheduled (none scheduled); the template has no lists for it.
- Gynaecology major cases: the template split (hysterectomy 40%, endometriosis excision 50%, myomectomy 10%) differs from the demand mix (hysterectomy 61%, endometriosis excision 24%, myomectomy 15%), so meeting demand for some procedures over-schedules others. Review the split in the advanced mapping table, or the demand.
